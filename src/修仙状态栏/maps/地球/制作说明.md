## 最终提示词

Use case: style-transfer. Asset: Earth world map for a Chinese cultivation game's map viewer. Image 1 is the geographic edit target: preserve its real Earth land shapes, coastlines, islands, relative positions and full global extent, Americas left, Eurasia and Africa center/right, Australia lower right and Antarctica along bottom. Image 2 is ONLY the ornamental frame and art-direction reference, never copy its fictional geography. Recreate image 1 as an elegant detailed physical world atlas, lightly aged parchment, subdued green and ochre land relief and teal blue oceans. Add a thin antique gold Chinese cloud-scroll border and decorative corners matching image 2, a narrow upper-left vertical title plaque reading exactly '地球全图', a small upper-right compass rose. Wide landscape approximately 2:1, high detail. Keep all Earth continents fully visible within the border. Small clear Chinese labels at geographic continent centers: 北美洲, 南美洲, 欧洲, 亚洲, 非洲, 大洋洲, 南极洲; ocean labels 太平洋 (both map edges), 大西洋, 印度洋, 北冰洋. No political borders, no fictional terrain, no cities, no legend panels, no inset boxes, no extra text, no watermark. Geographic fidelity takes priority over embellishment.
